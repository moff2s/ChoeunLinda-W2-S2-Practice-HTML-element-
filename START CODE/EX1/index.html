Welcome to Linda's blog! I am a second year student majoring in SE. I'm interested in fashion tech and thats why i am here.

Why Fashion Tech?

I'm interested in fashion tech because fashion is something everyone uses every day, yet the technology behind it is still behind. Many brands overproduce clothes, waste materials, and struggle to predict what customers want. As a software engineering student, I see a chance to help fix that with smart apps, data, and AI. I also love that it mixes creativity with code: I can build real tools while working with design and style. Fashion tech lets me solve practical problems in an industry people actually care about.
