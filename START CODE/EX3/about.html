Sambo Samnang (360) has been a familiar name in our Phnom Penh neighborhood,helping the local community with everyday financial needs. What started as a small money exchange counter has grown into a trusted one-stop shop where neighbors, students, and small business owners come for quick, dependable service.

We believe good service means being honest about rates, fast with transactions,and friendly no matter how busy the shop gets. Whether you're sending money to family, exchanging currency before a trip, or just topping up your phone, we treat every visit the same way — with care and respect.

Today, we're proud to be a go-to spot for our community — not just for money services, but for the little everyday things people need.
